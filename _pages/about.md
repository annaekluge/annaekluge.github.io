I am a doctoral researcher at the [WZB Berlin Social Science Center](https://www.wzb.eu) and Humboldt University Berlin.

My dissertation investigates the provision of reproduction knowledge in comparative perspective: how much and what kind of reproduction knowledge is provided through sex education curricula, how this differs across contexts and over time, and what drives these differences. To advance research on comparative and longitudinal research on sex education, I have compiled a large-scale [database on sex education curricula in Austria, Germany, and Switzerland since 1970](https://doi.org/10.7802/2911).

At the WZB, I work in the Emmy-Noether research group [Varieties of Reproduction Regimes](https://wzb.eu/en/research/dynamics-of-social-inequalities/varieties-of-reproduction-regimes). One of our key outputs is the [International Reproduction Policy Database (IRPD)](https://irpd.wzb.eu) on five different reproduction policy fields for 33 countries since 1980. 

My research has been published in peer-reviewed journals. Recent publications include ['Sexuality Education in Comparative Perspective'](https://doi.org/10.1080/14681811.2024.2434759) (*Sex Education*, 2026), and ['Conceptualizing and Measuring State Regulation of Reproductive Processes: The IRPD'](https://doi.org/10.1177/09589287241302962) (Zagel et al., *Journal of European Social Policy*, 2025). A further article on the politics of sex education in Austria, Germany, and Switzerland 
is forthcoming in the *European Journal of Politics and Gender*.
