I am a doctoral researcher at the [WZB Berlin Social Science Center](https://www.wzb.eu) and Humboldt University Berlin , supervised by Prof. Dr. Hannah Zagel. 

My dissertation investigates the provision of reproduction knowledge, focusing on the policy and politics of sex education across Austria, Germany, and Switzerland. I inquire how much and which kind of reproduction knowledge is provided through sex education school curriucla, how this has differs between contexts and over time, and how we can understand the differences. To advance reserach on comparative and longitudinal reserach on sex education, I have compiled a large-scale [database containing sex education curricual in Austria, Germany, and Switzerland since 1970](https://doi.org/10.7802/2911).

At the WZB, I work in the Emmy-Noether research group [Varieties of Reproduction Regimes](https://wzb.eu/en/research/dynamics-of-social-inequalities/varieties-of-reproduction-regimes). One of our key outputs is the [International Reproduction Policy Database (IRPD)](irpd.wzb.eu) on five different reproduction policy fields for 33 countries since 1980. 

My research has been published in peer-reviewed journals. Recent publications include 
['Sexuality Education in Comparative Perspective'](https://doi.org/10.1080/14681811.2024.2434759) 
(*Sex Education*, 2026), and
['Conceptualizing and Measuring State Regulation of Reproductive Processes: The IRPD'](https://doi.org/10.1177/09589287241302962) 
(Zagel et al., *Journal of European Social Policy*, 2025).
A further article on the politics of sex education in Austria, Germany, and Switzerland 
is forthcoming in the *European Journal of Politics and Gender*.
